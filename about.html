About Me

I build & secure industrial systems

I'm Kalaichelvan S, an OT / ICS Security Engineer with a BSc in Digital & Cyber Forensic Science. I spent years building operational-technology products end-to-end at Auvetra Labs — radar, vision, energy and BLE systems running on Modbus, BACnet, KNX and MQTT. That builder's view of the attack surface is exactly what I now bring to securing PLC, SCADA, DCS and industrial networks, aligned to IEC 62443.
