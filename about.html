About Me

I take products from idea to the customer's site

I'm Kalaichelvan S, a Forward Deployed Engineer. As founder of Auvetra Labs, I led a team of six that designed, built and deployed five products end to end: edge computer vision, mmWave radar, power factor correction, BLE presence tracking and an RFID platform on a factory floor. I work directly with customers to understand how their work actually happens, then build across the whole stack, firmware, hardware, backends, dashboards and cloud, and deploy it on-site until it works for the people using it.
