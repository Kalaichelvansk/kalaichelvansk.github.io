My Story

From founding a startup to building & deploying with customers

I founded and built Auvetra Labs, a DPIIT recognized startup, with a mission to make buildings smarter and more energy efficient. Our flagship work centered on presence-sensing products — radar, vision and BLE systems that detect people and drive automated energy savings. Along the way, we were backed by grants from MeitY Startup Hub and EDII-TN, and were selected for the NVIDIA Inception Program.

As a generalist with a strong product focus, I led a cross-functional team of six — working alongside PCB designers, product designers, software developers, consultants, certification partners, OEMs and fabricators to take an idea from schematic all the way to a certified, deployable product, idea to MVP. I worked directly with clients to understand their environments and close the gap between technology and deployment.

That's the forward-deployed job: sit with the customer, understand how the work actually happens, and ship software that fits. At a coir-processing facility, that meant spotting that wages depended on self-reported quantities and building FlowTracker: RFID readers plus a web platform that counts verified scans instead. My BSc in Digital & Cyber Forensic Science means I also design with security in mind from the start.

The technology worked — our sensors performed flawlessly in real-world conditions. What we couldn't crack was product-market fit at scale; the wall was sales and marketing, not engineering. But it taught me what I do best: sitting with a customer, finding the real problem, and shipping the system that solves it.

Edge Native Vision Platform

Facility teams wanted occupancy data and automatic energy savings without new cameras or the cloud. We built a platform that reuses their existing CCTV over RTSP, trained custom YOLO models, and deployed it on edge GPUs on-site, wired into their BACnet/KNX lighting and HVAC.

How I Build & Deploy

From customer problem to running in production

I run every engagement the same way: understand the work on-site, build across the whole stack, deploy into the real environment, then keep improving it with the people who use it.

  • 01 Discover

    Go on-site, sit with operators and managers, and map how the work actually happens: the workflows, the data, the incentives, and where it breaks.

  • 02 Build

    Prototype fast across the whole stack (firmware, backend, database and dashboard) and put it in front of real users early.

  • 03 Deploy

    Install in the field, integrate with existing equipment and protocols (Modbus, BACnet, RTSP), and handle provisioning, OTA and rollout.

  • 04 Support & iterate

    Train the people who use it, watch real usage, and keep shipping fixes and features from customer feedback.
