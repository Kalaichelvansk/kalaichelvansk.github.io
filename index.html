My Story

From building OT systems to securing them

I founded and built Auvetra Labs, a DPIIT recognized startup, with a mission to make buildings smarter and more energy efficient. Our flagship work centered on presence-sensing products — radar, vision and BLE systems that detect people and drive automated energy savings. Along the way, we were backed by grants from MeitY Startup Hub and EDII-TN, and were selected for the NVIDIA Inception Program.

As a generalist with a strong product focus, I led a cross-functional team of six — working alongside PCB designers, product designers, software developers, consultants, certification partners, OEMs and fabricators to take an idea from schematic all the way to a certified, deployable product, idea to MVP. I worked directly with clients to understand their environments and close the gap between technology and deployment.

Building those systems end-to-end gave me something to understand and mitigate risks along with CIA in IT and AIC in OT and mission critical infrastructures: an insider's map of exactly where OT breaks — the protocols, the firmware, the trust boundaries and the field-to-cloud data flows. Paired with my BSc in Digital & Cyber Forensic Science and hands-on tooling — Wireshark, Nmap, Shodan and Nessus — it's the foundation for where I'm focused now: OT / ICS security, on the PLC → SCADA → DCS → industrial-networking path and aligned to IEC 62443.

The technology worked — our sensors performed flawlessly in real-world conditions. What we couldn't crack was product-market fit at scale; the wall was sales and marketing, not engineering. But it led me somewhere better: I secure the systems I once built — because I know their attack surface from the inside.

How I Secure OT

A repeatable loop from asset to hardened

I approach every industrial environment the same way — map the assets, analyze the traffic, assess the risk, and harden the system, aligned to IEC 62443.

  • 01 Map

    Inventory OT assets, protocols and data flows across the Purdue model — field devices, PLCs, SCADA/HMI and up to the enterprise network.

  • 02 Analyze

    Capture and dissect industrial traffic (Modbus, BACnet, MQTT) in Wireshark; discover devices and internet exposure with Nmap and Shodan.

  • 03 Assess

    Identify weaknesses — default credentials, unauthenticated protocols, unpatched firmware — with Nessus, prioritized and mapped to IEC 62443.

  • 04 Harden

    Segment networks into zones & conduits, enforce authentication and TLS, secure firmware and OTA, and design for least privilege.
