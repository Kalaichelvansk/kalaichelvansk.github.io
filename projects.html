RFID · Factory Floor

FlowTracker RFID Pickup Tracking

A coir facility paid wages on self-reported quantities. We built the RFID readers, firmware and web platform and deployed them on the factory floor to count verified scans instead, with fleet provisioning, OTA updates and automated reports.

Edge AI · Facilities

Edge-Native Vision Platform

Facility teams wanted occupancy data and automatic energy savings without new cameras or the cloud. We built a platform that reuses their existing CCTV over RTSP, trained custom YOLO models, and deployed it on edge GPUs on-site, wired into their BACnet/KNX lighting and HVAC.

mmWave · Smart Buildings

Radar Presence System — V1 & V2

Customers needed presence detection that PIR missed, such as people sitting still. We designed, built and deployed two hardware generations: tracking of up to 10 occupants, a zone configurator, and integrations with AWS, Alexa, BACnet/KNX and Home Assistant.

Energy · Industrial Sites

Power Factor Correction

Facilities were paying utility penalties for poor power factor. We built and deployed a LAN-only system on-site that reads their energy meters over Modbus, switches capacitor banks automatically, and logs everything for audit.

BLE Presence Tracking
